# Financing Intro

## Capital Flow Dynamics

|Market|Capital Flow Dynamics (How Capital Flows To/From Other Markets)|Primary Purpose|Key Instruments|Maturity / Horizon|Risk vs. Return Profile|
|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|:---|
|Forex (FX) Market|The Gateway: Capital flows here first to convert currency before moving cross-border into international stock, bond, or commodity markets.|Facilitating international trade and currency exchange.|Currency pairs (e.g., EUR/USD).|Spot transactions or forward contracts.|Low to High Risk (dependent on leverage).|
|Money Market|The Cash Parking Lot: Capital flows here **from banks and corporations as a safe haven during market volatility**. It flows out to Debt, Equity, or Private Markets when investors seek higher returns.|Managing short-term cash liquidity and working capital.|T-Bills, Commercial Paper, CDs, Repos.|**Short-term** (Overnight to < 1 year).|Low Risk, Low Return (highly liquid).|
|Debt / Bond Market|The **Stable Income** Base: Capital flows here from Money Markets (for better yields) or Equity Markets (during a "flight to safety" in economic downturns). **Capital flows out to Equities during expansions**.|Raising long-term capital for projects and infrastructure.|Government, corporate, and municipal bonds.|**Medium to Long-term** (1 to 30+ years).|Moderate Risk, Moderate Return.|
|Equity Market|The Growth Engine: Capital flows here from Debt and Money markets during economic growth ("risk-on"). It flows out to Debt/Money markets during market crashes ("risk-off").|Raising permanent capital by selling ownership stakes.|Common and preferred stock.|N/A|High Risk, High Return.|
|Private Markets (VC/PE)|The **Illiquid** Lockup: Capital flows here **from institutional portfolios seeking high returns**. It **flows out back to Public Equities (via IPOs) or cash (via acquisitions)** after several years.|Providing tailored funding to private, non-publicly traded companies.|Venture capital equity, buyouts, private loans.|Long-term (typically 5 to 10+ years).|Very High Risk, High Potential Return (illiquid).|
|Commodity Market|The Inflation Hedge: Capital flows here from Equity and Debt markets **during periods of high inflation or geopolitical tension**. It flows **back to financial assets once the economy stabilizes**.|Trading physical raw materials and hedging supply chain risks.|Hard (Gold, Oil) and Soft (Wheat) commodities.|Spot purchases or futures contracts.|Moderate to High Risk (volatile).|
|Derivatives Market|The Risk Overlay: Capital does not permanently reside here; instead, it flows in and out of derivatives to hedge risks or speculate on price movements in the underlying Equity, Debt, FX, or Commodity markets.|Hedging financial risks and speculating on price movements.|Futures, options, swaps, forwards.|Defined contract expiration dates.|High Risk, High Return (highly leveraged).|
